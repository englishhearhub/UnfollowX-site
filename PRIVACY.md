# Privacy Policy / 隐私政策

**UnfollowX** — Last updated / 更新日期：2026-10-12

## English

### Summary
UnfollowX does not collect, transmit, sell, or store any user data. Everything runs locally in your browser.

### What the extension does
- Reads your X (Twitter) session cookies (`ct0`, `twid`) locally, solely to call X's official APIs on your behalf (reading your following/follower lists, unfollowing, following). Cookies are sent only to x.com as part of those direct requests — never to any third party.
- Stores your preferences (pacing, batch size, skip rules, UI language) in your browser's local extension storage (`chrome.storage.local`). This data never leaves your device.

### What the extension does NOT do
- No data collection, no analytics, no tracking, no advertising.
- No external servers. The extension communicates only with x.com / twitter.com.
- No passwords, credentials, or personal content are requested, accessed, transmitted, sold, or stored by the developer.

### Changes
Any changes to this policy will be published at this URL.

## 中文

### 概述
UnfollowX 不收集、不传输、不出售、不存储任何用户数据。所有功能均在你的浏览器本地运行。

### 扩展会做什么
- 在本地读取你的 X（Twitter）会话 Cookie（`ct0`、`twid`），仅用于以你本人的身份调用 X 官方接口（读取关注/粉丝列表、执行取关/回关）。Cookie 只随这些直接请求发送至 x.com，绝不发往任何第三方。
- 将你的设置（操作间隔、批次大小、跳过规则、界面语言）保存在浏览器本地扩展存储（`chrome.storage.local`）中，数据不会离开你的设备。

### 扩展不会做什么
- 不收集数据、不做分析、不做追踪、不放广告。
- 没有任何外部服务器，仅与 x.com / twitter.com 通信。
- 开发者不要求、不访问、不传输、不出售、不存储你的密码、凭据或任何个人内容。

本政策如有变更，将在此页面公布。
